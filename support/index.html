What does Purlwise cost?

Purlwise needs Purlwise Pro: a yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You'll see the current prices in your currency before you buy.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Purlwise. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. You can also get there from Purlwise: Settings, then Manage subscription.

I already paid. How do I get Purlwise Pro back?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought with, then tap Restore purchases on the Unlock Purlwise screen, or in Purlwise's Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I connect or disconnect Ravelry?

Tap the person button at the top left of Projects or Stash to open Settings, then choose Ravelry. Connect opens Ravelry's own sign-in page; Purlwise never sees your password. Disconnect removes the connection from your iPhone. Connecting is optional, and everything else works without it.

Where are my projects saved?

On your iPhone. Projects, counters, notes, stash, photos and PDFs stay on the device and are included in your iPhone or iCloud backups if you use them. Deleting Purlwise deletes them, and we have no copy we could restore.

How do I add my own PDF pattern?

Open a project, go to its pattern section, and choose Import from Files. The PDF is copied into Purlwise, so it opens even offline, with the row ruler and counters on top.

Does Purlwise work offline?

Yes. Your projects, PDFs and counters work without a connection. Catalog photos you haven't opened yet need a connection to load the first time.

Privacy

Purlwise has no accounts, ads or tracking. Superwall, which shows the paywall, receives your purchase history and an anonymous ID. Read the full privacy policy.
